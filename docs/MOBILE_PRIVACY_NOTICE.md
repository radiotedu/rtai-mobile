# RadioTEDU Mobile Privacy Notice

Effective date: 22 August 2026
Version: 2026-08-22

This notice applies to the RadioTEDU Android, Android Auto, Android Automotive,
Wear OS, Android TV, and command-line applications. The in-app notice is
available in English, Turkish, Arabic, Russian, German, and French.

## Data processed

Depending on the features used, RadioTEDU may process:

- account and profile information, including email address, display name,
  profile image, account role, and information supplied during registration;
- authentication, session, ERP-link, security, fraud-prevention, and support
  records;
- service activity, including favourites, podcast progress, Study activity,
  Voting and Jukebox requests, game results, Gold activity, event participation,
  and market requests;
- stream, podcast, station, language, app-version, device/platform, crash, and
  diagnostic information needed to deliver and secure the service;
- notification tokens and preferences when notifications are enabled; and
- optional analytics and self-declared demographic selections only after the
  corresponding consent is enabled.

Passwords are submitted to the authentication service and are not intentionally
included in analytics events. RadioTEDU does not sell personal data and does not
use app analytics for targeted advertising.

## Purposes and legal bases

Essential data is used to provide requested app, account, playback, Study,
Voting, Jukebox, game, Gold, event, and support functions; maintain security;
prevent abuse; honour account deletion and rights requests; and comply with
legal duties. Depending on the processing, the legal basis is performance of the
service terms, a legal obligation, or proportionate legitimate interests in
operating and securing RadioTEDU. Consent is used where the app explicitly asks
for it, including optional Google Analytics and optional demographics.

Declining optional analytics does not disable essential app functions. Consent
can be withdrawn in Profile > Data & Privacy. Withdrawal stops future optional
collection, clears demographic properties, and resets the local analytics
identity; records already received remain subject to the retention and rights
processes described below.

## Recipients and external services

Access is limited to authorised TED University/RadioTEDU personnel and providers
needed to operate the service. Depending on use, recipients may include hosting,
email, identity, security, notification, and analytics providers, and public
authorities where legally required.

- Google Analytics for Firebase is off by default. If enabled, Google may
  receive an app-instance identifier, app/session and screen activity,
  listening/channel identifiers and duration, app/device/platform/language
  information, and approximate location derived by Google from a masked IP
  address. Optional age range and gender are sent only when separately enabled.
  Account name, email, advertising ID, contacts, GPS, and precise location are
  not attached by the app to analytics events.
- When streamed metadata contains artist and song text, the app may send that
  search text to Apple's public iTunes Search API to find artwork. Apple also
  receives ordinary connection data such as IP address. RadioTEDU account tokens
  and email addresses are not sent in this request.
- Playback and first-party web features connect to RadioTEDU/TED University
  infrastructure, which receives ordinary connection and request logs.

Providers may process information outside Türkiye or the EEA. Where required,
RadioTEDU relies on applicable contractual, organisational, and legal transfer
safeguards. Google and Apple publish their own privacy terms.

## Retention and security

Account data is retained while the account and requested service are active.
Feature, security, support, and transaction records are retained only for the
period needed for their purpose and then, where applicable, for statutory,
dispute, fraud-prevention, security, and backup periods. Deletion requests remove
or de-identify data unless a legal or security obligation requires limited
retention. Optional analytics records follow the retention configured for the
RadioTEDU Google Analytics property.

RadioTEDU applies access controls, encrypted transport, limited client
permissions, consent-gated analytics, and session controls appropriate to the
service. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

## Your rights

Subject to applicable KVKK and GDPR rules, you may request information and
access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection, and portability where
applicable. You may withdraw consent at any time without affecting processing
that was lawful before withdrawal. Account deletion is available in the app's
Profile area. Send other requests to radio@tedu.edu.tr. Identity verification may
be required before fulfilling a request.

You may also complain to the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK)
or, where applicable, your competent EEA supervisory authority.

## Changes

Material changes will be identified by a new version/date and, where required,
shown in the app before continued use or renewed consent.
